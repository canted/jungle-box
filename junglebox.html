Playing the instrument

Tap Fill for a run into the bar line. Tap again to cut it short. Hold Catch to repeat a fragment. Hold Drop to shed the kick and expose the shuffle. Release for a pickup into the next beat. Hold Home to store the groove, tap to recall it. Tap Home (H) four times evenly to set tempo (60–240 BPM). Keep taps short; holds, other controls, and pauses clear the tap sequence. Space controls transport. Use ↑ / ↓ to select a break and + / − (or =) for volume. Hold G/Gesture and use Select break ↑ / ↓ to raise/lower tempo by 1 BPM without changing breaks or the percussion loop. Keep ↑ / ↓ held to repeat, slowly at first and then faster; release either control to stop. Arrow keys adjust a fader when it is focused.
